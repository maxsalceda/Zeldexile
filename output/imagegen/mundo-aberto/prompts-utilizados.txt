01 — Vista geral do vale
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original open-world video game environment mockup, first of three coherent views of one new world.
Primary request: create a lush inviting green open world for an original anime adventure game. Take only broad visual inspiration from the painterly cel-shaded open-world aesthetics of Breath of the Wild and Genshin Impact; invent the geography, landmarks and environment design from scratch.
Scene: a broad sunlit valley of rolling grassy meadows, scattered fruit-tree groves, a winding shallow turquoise river feeding a large freshwater lake, small wooden footbridges and walkable earth paths. Distinctive original landscape identity: low pale honey-limestone ridges layered in broad gentle curves, with a distant saddle-shaped ridge whose two peaks have unequal heights; airy fruit trees with exposed twisting reddish-brown branches and broad umbrella-shaped crowns, some bearing small round coral-orange fruits and others golden pear-shaped fruits. A few natural fallen branches lie beside the paths, never block the main trail. Mature trees are organically spaced rather than planted in rigid rows. Rich emerald, meadow green and yellow-green grass with occasional tiny white wildflowers. Natural reeds and smooth stones at the shoreline.
Composition: ONE expansive cinematic landscape image, 16:9. An elevated camera from a low grassy hillside, looking across connected explorable terrain. A wide, visibly continuous dirt trail leads through foreground fruit trees to a modest supported wooden bridge across the river, then branches toward a lakeside orchard and a higher meadow. Make the route connections, scale changes and open spaces plausible for an actual playable game. Foreground, middle distance and far distance clearly layered. Beautiful readable tree silhouettes frame the view without obscuring most of the valley. The lake and distinctive limestone ridge are memorable landmarks. The geography should feel spacious and continuous, extending beyond the frame.
Style: high-quality stylized 3D anime RPG environment render, painterly color variation, clean graphic foliage masses, controlled cel-shading, attractive simplified forms, crisp midground details and soft blue atmospheric perspective. Bright late-morning sunlight, luminous but controlled colors, broad soft clouds, calm inviting exploration mood. Terrain and foliage must look like game art, not photography or a realistic architectural rendering.
Constraints: landscape only, no characters, no interface, no map overlay, no text or labels, no watermark, no montage or panels. No recognizable franchise landmarks, monuments, symbols, architecture or copied map layouts. No fantasy castles, giant statues, floating islands or industrial objects. Wooden bridges must connect real banks and have sensible supports and plank construction. Full opaque environment image.
